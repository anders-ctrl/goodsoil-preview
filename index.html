Why this matters

People want better food. Discovery still feels broken.

Consumers want provenance, quality, and trust. Producers exist. Demand exists. The current discovery experience is still fragmented, dated, and weirdly hard.

Consumer shift

People want proof, not vague supermarket claims.

The rise of provenance, farm shops, butchers, premium ingredient culture, and cleaner eating points to a wider shift: people want to know where food came from and whether it is worth paying more for.

Trust Clear source beats anonymous supermarket abstraction.
Taste Better produce, meat, dairy, and eggs make the extra effort feel worth it.
Status Local food is becoming culturally cooler, not just ethically worthy.
